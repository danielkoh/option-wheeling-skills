# Tom Lee (Fundstrat) — Top-Down Market Strategy

Tom Lee is a **market strategist**, not a single-stock value investor. His framework is
**top-down**: form a view on the market/index and on sectors/themes, driven by macro,
liquidity, earnings, demographics, and **sentiment**. Use this lens for index targets,
regime calls, sector rotation, and thematic baskets — *complementary* to the bottom-up
quality/value lenses (Buffett/Lynch/Ackman), which size individual names.

## Core framework
1. **Earnings + valuation regime.** Markets follow earnings over time; he anchors S&P targets
   on forward EPS × a justified P/E, where the multiple is conditioned on **rates, inflation,
   and liquidity**. Falling inflation / easing financial conditions support multiple
   expansion; rising real rates compress it.
2. **Liquidity & financial conditions.** Fed policy, real rates, and money flows are primary
   drivers of risk appetite. Easing conditions → risk-on; tightening → de-rate.
3. **Demographics / structural demand.** A long-running thesis: the **millennial cohort**
   (the largest generation entering peak earning/spending years) structurally supports demand,
   housing, and equities into the 2030s — a secular tailwind.
4. **Sentiment as a contrarian tool.** Extreme pessimism/positioning is often a **buy** signal;
   euphoria a caution. He's known for **constructive/bullish** contrarian calls when consensus
   is fearful ("the most hated bull market"). Gauge positioning, surveys, put/call, breadth.
5. **Seasonality, breadth & technicals overlay.** Uses seasonal patterns, market breadth
   (participation), and key technical levels to **time** the fundamental view.
6. **Themes & baskets, not single names.** Expresses views through **thematic baskets** and
   sectors (e.g. his "Granny Shots" style high-conviction multi-theme overlap, tech/AI,
   financials, energy at points, and **Bitcoin** as a scarce, adoption-driven asset with a
   long-horizon target). Diversify the *theme*, not just the stock.

## What Tom Lee emphasizes
- **Stay constructive unless the macro regime breaks** — long-run equity drift is up; don't
  fight earnings + demographics + liquidity when they align.
- **Buy fear, respect positioning.** Capitulation + washed-out sentiment + improving liquidity
  is his classic setup.
- **Catalysts & calendar**: Fed meetings, CPI prints, earnings season, seasonality windows.
- **Bitcoin / digital assets** as a structural adoption + scarcity play (high-beta, volatile).

## Red flags / cautions in his framework
- Tightening liquidity + rising real rates + extended valuations + euphoric sentiment = de-risk.
- Deteriorating earnings revisions and narrowing breadth (few stocks holding up the index).
- He is structurally **optimistic**; treat his calls as a *bull-case macro overlay* and stress
  it against bear scenarios — don't confuse a directional strategist's view with a guarantee.

## How to apply
Use Tom Lee's lens for the **top-down context** around a trade: where are we in the
earnings/valuation/liquidity regime, what's sentiment/positioning saying (contrarian read),
which **themes/sectors** have the tailwind, and what near-term **catalysts** (Fed/CPI/earnings/
seasonality) matter. Then hand off to the bottom-up lenses to pick and size the individual
names that express the theme. Frame outputs as **index/sector/theme targets with a scenario
range**, not point predictions.
