# Price Action: Levels, Patterns, Candles

> Patterns are *subjective* and have modest unconditional base rates. Their real value is
> **risk definition** — they give a clear invalidation level — not prophecy. Always require
> **confirmation** (a close beyond the level, ideally on volume) and define the stop.

## Support & resistance
- Zones where prior supply/demand clustered: swing highs/lows, prior consolidation, round
  numbers, the open/prior close, moving averages, VWAP, Fibonacci retracements (38.2/50/61.8%).
- **Polarity**: broken resistance often becomes support and vice-versa.
- More touches + more volume + more time = stronger level. Trade the *reaction* at the
  level, not the level blindly.

## Trend
- Uptrend = higher highs + higher lows; downtrend = lower highs + lower lows. A trend is
  intact until that structure breaks. Trendlines/channels are aids, not laws.

## Continuation patterns (trend likely resumes)
- **Flags / pennants**: brief counter-trend consolidation after a sharp move ("pole").
- **Triangles**: ascending (bullish bias), descending (bearish), symmetrical (neutral —
  trade the break). Measured move ≈ height of the pattern projected from the breakout.
- **Cup & handle**: rounded base + small pullback; bullish continuation.

## Reversal patterns
- **Head & shoulders** (and inverse): three peaks, middle highest; neckline break confirms.
  Target ≈ head-to-neckline distance projected from the break.
- **Double/triple top & bottom**: failure to make a new extreme; confirmed on the
  intervening level break.

## Candlesticks (single/2-bar context signals)
- **Doji** = indecision; **hammer / shooting star** = rejection wicks at extremes;
  **bullish/bearish engulfing** = momentum shift; **morning/evening star** = 3-bar reversal.
- Candles matter most **at a level** and **with confirmation** — a hammer in mid-air is noise.

## Divergence (often the highest-quality signal)
- **Regular**: price makes a new high/low, oscillator (RSI/MACD) does not → weakening
  momentum, possible reversal.
- **Hidden**: price makes a higher low but oscillator a lower low → trend-continuation signal.

## Discipline
1. Identify the level/pattern and its **invalidation** (where you're wrong).
2. Wait for **confirmation** (close beyond, volume).
3. Size off ATR; set a **measured-move target** and a stop; state the **R:R**.
4. Accept that any single setup is a probability, not a certainty — the edge is in
   repetition with positive expectancy and disciplined risk.
